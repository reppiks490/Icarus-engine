## 0. Data integrity fix I-1 (`cl_lab/integrity.py`, data version `cl-data-2`)

Finding (2026-10-04): `data/mnq_5m_full.csv` and `data/mnq_20m_full.csv` are **unadjusted continuous MNQ series**. The contract switches during the session dated the Thursday eight days before each quarterly expiry. The MNQ − NASDAQ-100 basis (16:00 ET closes) jumps by +216 to +287 points on exactly those eight sessions (2024-12-12, 2025-03-13, 2025-06-12, 2025-09-11, 2025-12-11, 2026-03-12, 2026-06-11, 2026-09-10) and moves a median 7 points on other days.

Impact on `cl-data-1` results: RTH-only trades are unaffected. On roll sessions the features `prev_close`, `prev_high`, `prev_low`, `on_high`, `on_low`, the gap, the prior-close-to-decision returns and the ATR true range are wrong, and so is any position held across the switch (THE PULSE track trades through it).

Fix (detector amended before evaluation: on 3 of the 8 switch sessions the tape has a 4–5-hour data gap, so a single bar jump mixes the spread with the market's move across the gap):
1. Primary detector: the basis is the last RTH close minus the FRED NASDAQ-100 close. For each quarter (third Friday F of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, unshifted), the switch session is the session dated in [F − 14 d, F − 1 d] with the largest |basis change|. It is accepted at ≥ 40 bp of price, and the adjustment Δ equals that basis change. The switch bar is the session's largest bar-to-bar jump when that jump has Δ's sign and at least |Δ|/2. Otherwise the whole session counts as the new contract, and its overnight high and low are blanked. Fallback when the index is unavailable: the largest bar jump in the session dated F − 8 d (a Thursday), accepted at ≥ 40 bp. A quarter with no accepted switch is `ROLL_NOT_DETECTED`, and multi-session holds spanning [F − 14 d, F] are skipped.
2. On this tape all 8 switches are detected. Six are bar switches at 00:00 ET, and two (2025-03, 2025-12) are session-boundary switches. Δ = +216 to +287 points (88–122 bp). The 20-minute PULSE tape takes the same sessions and Δ, with its switch bar relocated by the same rule.
3. Panama additive back-adjustment: every bar before an accepted switch is shifted by that switch's Δ (cumulative over later switches). Point differences inside each contract are preserved exactly. Percentage features shift by at most ΣΔ / price (under 10 % early in the tape, under 1 % in the HOLD window).
4. Every run records the detected switches (session date, timestamp, Δ points, Δ bp), the switch bar's own jump, and the method used. When the FRED NASDAQ-100 feed is cached, the run also records a basis cross-check. The check runs on the back-adjusted closes. Any session-to-session basis change above 150 points sets integrity status `DEGRADED` in the run output, because it means either a missed roll or a data error. It is never silently ignored.
5. cl-g1, explorer, cl-r1 and PULSE are re-evaluated on `cl-data-2` under their existing ids. That is a data correction, not new trials, and status changes appear in `status_history`.

## 1. Shared machinery

- `cl_lab/events.py`: verified scheduled FOMC decision dates for 2024–2026. Every meeting is two days, the first day is the previous business day, and each one had a press conference. The 2025-08-22 notation vote is excluded. The module also holds quarterly expiries and month-end trading-day positions taken from the tape's own session list.
- `cl_lab/multisession.py`: a time-exit hold simulator. Entry happens at the open of slot s0 on session d0, or at that session's 16:00 close when s0 = 78. Exit is defined the same way on session d1, which comes later. A trade is skipped when any referenced bar is missing. P&L is marked to market at each session's last RTH close, so the daily values telescope to the trade total. The round-trip cost is charged half on the entry session and half on the exit session. A hold that spans a roll session without adjustment is skipped as `spans_roll`.
- Daily index simulator, used by R2-B1: a weight w_t is held from close t−1 to close t, the return is w_t·r_t, and the cost is |w_t − w_{t−1}| times the per-side cost.
- Session rules: intraday-entry rules (R2-A, R2-D, cl-ml1) use complete sessions only, meaning all 78 RTH closes are present. For multi-session holds, "the close" means the session's last available RTH close, which handles early-close days.
- Windows: TUNE runs to 2025-10-01 (exclusive). HOLD runs from 2025-10-01 up to `FORWARD_START` = 2026-10-05 (exclusive). FORWARD is everything after that. All three are unchanged from cl-gates-1.

## 2. Hypothesis families (version `cl-r2`)

### R2-A End-of-day reversal (MNQ only), HOLD-confirmation protocol `cl-hc1`

r_d = C[d, 15:25 bar] / last RTH close of session d−1 − 1 (back-adjusted). Enter −sign(r_d) at the open of the 15:30 bar and exit at the 16:00 close. No trade when r_d = 0 or any input is missing.

| id stem | condition (all trailing, causal) |
|---|---|
| A1 `unconditional` | none |
| A2 `r_top_tercile` | \|r_d\| ≥ 2/3-quantile of the prior 250 sessions' \|r\| (min 60) |
| A3 `rv_open_top_tercile` | std of the five 5-minute log returns of 09:30–10:00 ≥ 2/3-quantile of the prior 250 (min 60) |
| A4 `vix_low` | prior-day Cboe VIX close ≤ the median of its prior 250 values, with at least 20 prior values (`conditions.mask(sess, "vix_low")` as implemented). This is a proxy for dealer long gamma: Baltussen–Da–Lammers–Martens find intraday momentum under short gamma, which implies reversal under long gamma |

Known before registration: the momentum mirror of A1–A3 had TUNE NW t = −2.221, −1.653 and −1.514, net of costs, on `cl-data-1` (cl-r1). The A4 mirror was never computed. HOLD was never computed for any variant.

Decision (HOLD only, base cost $2.70 per round turn): NW t ≥ 1.65, a Holm-adjusted one-sided p ≤ 0.05 across A1–A4, at least 30 HOLD trades, and a HOLD mean > 0 at stress cost. A variant that passes becomes `HOLD_CONFIRMED`, a forward-shadow candidate. It can never become CHAMPION from this protocol. Promotion needs at least 60 FORWARD trades with forward NW t ≥ 1.65. A variant that fails becomes `REJECTED`. TUNE statistics are reported for transparency only.

### R2-B Month-end rebalancing pressure (Harvey–Mazzoleni–Melone, NBER w33554)

**B1, daily, FRED `NASDAQ100` (1986-01-02 onward) plus FRED `DGS10`.** For month m, M0 is the last trading day of m−1 and T is the last trading day of m. Positions are taken from the tape's dates.
- Equity leg: R_eq = NDX[T−6] / NDX[M0] − 1.
- Bond leg: the daily par-bond return is r_b(t) = −D(y_{t−1})·(y_t − y_{t−1})/100 + y_{t−1}/(100·252), with D(y) = (1 − (1 + y/200)^−20)/(y/100). R_bd compounds r_b over DGS10 dates in (M0, T−7]. The two-day lag covers H.15 publication timing. This bond return is an approximation, declared as such.
- Signal: z = (R_eq − R_bd) / (σ·√n). σ is the standard deviation of the paired daily (r_eq − r_b) over the 60 trading days ending T−7, and n is the number of trading days in (M0, T−6].
- Entry at the close of T−5, so the inputs are at least one session old. T−k is the k-th NDX trading day before T in the NDX date list. The first month of the tape is skipped because it has no M0. σ uses the dates present in both series.
- DSR deflation uses the family's 3 pre-registered trials and their measured Sharpe dispersion. This is light deflation, and it is honest only because nothing else was tried on this tape.

| variant | position |
|---|---|
| B1a `window5_z1` | −sign(z) when \|z\| > 1, held for days T−4..T (exit at the close of T) |
| B1b `next1_z1` | same signal, held for day T−4 only |
| B1c `window5_linear` | w = −clip(z/2, −1, 1) every month, held for days T−4..T |

Costs: base 1.0 bp per unit round trip, stress 3.0 bp. Gates: `validate.evaluate_external` with the same thresholds, n_trials = 3, and var_sr = the cross-variant TUNE Sharpe variance. Reported descriptively, not as gates: 1986–1996 (before the paper's sample), 1997–2023 (the paper's sample) and 2024 onward (after it).

**B2, MNQ execution twin (descriptive only, too few events to gate).** The B1a signal is computed from NDX and DGS10 as above. MNQ (back-adjusted) is entered at the 16:00 close of T−5 and exited at the 16:00 close of T, with MNQ costs. This is reported so the forward shadow can trade the same rule.

Known before registration: the published result (S&P 500 and T-note futures, 1997–2023). CL has computed nothing on NDX or MNQ month-ends.

### R2-C Pre-FOMC drift (MNQ), event study

Long from the open of the 14:00 ET bar on the first meeting day to the open of the 13:55 ET bar on decision day. Both sessions must be consecutive in the tape. The tape holds 16 events (8 TUNE, 8 HOLD). Reported per event: points, bp and net USD, plus the mean, NW t, sign-test p and a stationary-bootstrap 90 % CI of the mean. Status is **`INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` by construction**: G1's 60 trades take about 8 years at 8 events a year. The study keeps accumulating forward.

Known before registration: the literature's decay (Kurov–Wolfe–Gilbert 2021). Nothing computed by CL.

### R2-D Crypto aggressive-flow imbalance (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT; Binance 5m `taker_buy_volume`)

imb_w(d) = Σ_w (2·TB − V) / Σ_w V over RTH slots w of the New York clock (weekdays). The trade is taken only when |imb_w| ≥ the 2/3-quantile of the prior 250 values (min 60). Exit at the 16:00 ET close.

| variant | window, entry | direction |
|---|---|---|
| D1 `open30_cont` | 09:30–10:00, open of 10:00 bar | sign(imb) |
| D2 `open30_rev` | same | −sign(imb) |
| D3 `morning_cont` | 09:30–12:00, open of 12:00 bar | sign(imb) |
| D4 `morning_rev` | same | −sign(imb) |

The gates are the full cl-gates-1 stack per asset (`validate.evaluate`, returns metric, CRYPTO_PERP base and stress costs), with this family's own FDR and DSR. The prior is weak: order-imbalance persistence in equities (Chordia–Subrahmanyam 2004) against price-pressure reversal. The signal comes from spot flow and execution assumes perpetuals. Known before registration: nothing.

## 3. Walk-forward ML family `cl-ml1` (`cl_lab/ml.py`)

The aim is a regularized combination of causal features at fixed decision times. It is one pre-registered strategy per (asset, decision time) and passes through the same gates. Every prediction is out-of-sample by construction, in TUNE as well as HOLD.

- Decision slots k at 10:00, 12:00 and 15:30 ET make 3 candidates per asset, 9 in total.
- Features on session d at slot k come only from bars that closed before slot k opened and from sessions before d:
  1. C[d,k−1] / last close of d−1 − 1
  2. C[d,k−1] / O[d,0] − 1
  3. O[d,0] / last close of d−1 − 1
  4. std of the log returns over slots 0..k−1
  5. (max H − min L over slots 0..k−1) / atr14
  6. (C[d,k−1] − vwap[d,k−1]) / atr14
  7. Σ V over slots 0..k−1 / the mean of the same sum over the prior 20 sessions
  8. last close of d−1 / last close of d−2 − 1
  9. MNQ: log of the prior-day VIX. Crypto: the imbalance over slots 0..k−1
  10. MNQ: prior-day VIX9D / VIX − 1. Crypto: the full-RTH imbalance of d−1

  A spec whose feature is unavailable in a run is reported `UNAVAILABLE`. It is never imputed.
- Label: 1 if C[d,77] > O[d,k], otherwise 0.
- Model: logistic regression minimizing the mean log loss + (α/2)·‖β‖² with α = 0.1, on features z-scored with the training window's mean and std. The intercept is unpenalized. It is fit by Newton–IRLS (at most 25 iterations, tolerance 1e-8).
- Walk-forward: an expanding window, refit every 21 sessions. Training rows are sessions with an index below the block start minus 1 (a 1-session embargo). The first fit needs at least 120 complete rows, and the model predicts only the block that follows.
- Trade: long at O[d,k] when p ≥ 0.52, short when p ≤ 0.48, exit at the 16:00 close. Only complete sessions (all 78 RTH closes present) are used for training and trading.
- Qualification report (not gates): OOS Brier score against training-window climatology, log loss, AUC, calibration slope, coefficient sign stability across refits, and the same-window net P&L of two baselines (always long from O[d,k], and sign of feature 1). It also runs a seeded label-permutation control. If the control reaches |TUNE t| ≥ 3, the run is flagged `HARNESS_SUSPECT`.
- CI leakage tests: prefix invariance (predictions and trades for sessions before K are unchanged when later sessions are removed), intraday garbage-after-cut invariance, and a leaky positive control (a feature that uses C[d,77]) that the harness must flag.

Options considered and not adopted: adaptive (bandit) explorer sampling raises selection bias faster than it raises power at this sample size. Tree ensembles and neural networks are inappropriate with about 500 sessions per asset and no new dependencies. Meta-labeling over rules that all failed TUNE has no base edge to filter.
